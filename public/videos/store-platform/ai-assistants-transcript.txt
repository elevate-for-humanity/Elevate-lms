Meet Your AI Support Team
PARIS AI presenter · October 25, 2026

Too many questions, too many documents, and not enough time? This is Elevate for Humanity, and I am PARIS, your AI presenter. Today, meet the assistants described in Elevate's store. PARIS focuses on sales and intake. ELLIE focuses on learning and support. LIZZY focuses on operations. ZORA focuses on compliance assistance. Think of a training organization receiving an inquiry. Intake guidance can help organize the person's questions. Learning support can point a learner toward relevant course information. Operations assistance can help staff organize documents and next steps. Compliance assistance can help prepare material for a qualified human to review. These are examples of how to use the tools, not a claim that every task happens automatically. AI does not decide grant eligibility, award licenses, or replace professional judgment. Use approved materials, protect private information, and check outputs before acting. Choose one repetitive task this week, write down its current steps, and test whether an assistant makes the work clearer. Compare the result against your normal process. Visit the store to inspect the assistants, their current prices, and their limits. Follow the show for more practical demonstrations.
