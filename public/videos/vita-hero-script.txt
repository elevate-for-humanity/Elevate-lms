RISE UP FOUNDATION — VITA HERO VIDEO SCRIPT
Nonprofit Free Tax Preparation
Duration: 60–90 seconds
Tone: Warm, community-centered, empowering

---

SCENE 1 — OPEN (0:00–0:08)
Visual: Community center or library exterior. Families arriving. Volunteers
greeting clients at the door. Welcoming atmosphere.
No voiceover. Soft, hopeful music.

SCENE 2 — THE REALITY (0:08–0:18)
Visual: A single parent reviewing bills at a kitchen table. A senior citizen
with a stack of tax forms. A young worker with their first W-2.
Voiceover (warm, unhurried):
"Every year, millions of Americans overpay on their taxes — or leave money on
the table — simply because they can't afford professional help."

SCENE 3 — THE MISSION (0:18–0:32)
Visual: Volunteer sitting with a client, going through documents together.
Genuine conversation. Diverse faces — seniors, young adults, families.
Voiceover:
"Rise Up Foundation changes that. Through the IRS Volunteer Income Tax Assistance
program, our certified volunteers prepare your complete federal and state tax return —
at absolutely no cost to you."

SCENE 4 — THE PROCESS (0:32–0:48)
Visual: Volunteer reviewing a return on screen. Client nodding. E-file
confirmation. Client leaving with a smile.
Voiceover:
"No hidden fees. No upsells. No pressure. Just accurate, quality-reviewed tax
preparation by IRS-certified volunteers who are here because they want to help."

SCENE 5 — EITC MOMENT (0:48–1:00)
Visual: Volunteer pointing to a screen showing a refund amount. Client's
surprised, happy reaction.
Voiceover:
"Many of our clients qualify for the Earned Income Tax Credit — a refund worth
thousands of dollars that too many people miss when filing on their own."

SCENE 6 — CTA (1:00–1:15)
Visual: VITA site location. Volunteers and clients together. Community feel.
Voiceover:
"If your household income is $67,000 or below, you may qualify for free tax
preparation through Rise Up Foundation. Find a site near you at
supersonicfastermoney.com — and keep every dollar you've earned."

---

PRODUCTION NOTES:
- No text overlays on hero footage
- Use real volunteers and real clients (with consent) where possible
- Avoid stock-photo feel — authentic community setting is essential
- Music: warm, hopeful, not dramatic
- Color grade: natural, bright, welcoming
- End card: Rise Up Foundation logo + website only
- Clearly separate from Supersonic Fast Cash branding
