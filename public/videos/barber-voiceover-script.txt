USAGE NOTES
- Do not claim "state-approved institution" or "approved by Indiana DOE"
- Do not claim ETPL status unless confirmed — use "workforce funding aligned" instead
- DOL Registered Apprenticeship Sponsor status is accurate and can be stated
- Indiana barber license exam reference is accurate — apprentices sit for the state exam
  independently; Elevate does not issue the license
