# HVAC Certification Syllabus

**Provider:** Elevate for Humanity
**Training plan:** Six weeks, with four weeks of scheduled online study followed by two weeks of supervised hands-on training.
**Hands-on instructor:** David Nazaire. Confirm appointment dates, location, and required protective equipment with your instructor before attending.

## Training schedule

| Week | Focus | Work to complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Orientation, shop safety, tools, and HVAC system components | Complete the assigned lessons and the orientation and system-operation workbook sections. |
| 2 | Electrical fundamentals, heating and cooling systems, and the refrigeration cycle | Work through the assigned workbook diagrams and the virtual refrigeration lab. Explain where heat moves and how the component states differ. |
| 3 | EPA Section 608 Core and Type I preparation | Complete the assigned refrigerant safety and small-appliance lessons, workbook reviews, and knowledge checks. |
| 4 | EPA Section 608 Types II and III and cumulative practice | Complete the assigned high- and low-pressure lessons and workbook reviews. Bring questions from the practice assessments to your instructor. |
| 5 | Supervised hands-on practice | Attend your confirmed training session. Practice identifying components, using approved tools, and recording observations under supervision. |
| 6 | Supervised diagnostics and review | Complete assigned practical exercises and review outstanding evidence and exam readiness with your instructor. |

Use the dates in your portal and the instructor's confirmed appointments. Your assigned lessons and workbook show the work due for your enrollment.

## Course and workbook

- Open the assigned **EPA 608 Refrigeration — Core, Types I, II & III** course from **My Courses**.
- Use the **HVAC and EPA 608 Illustrated Practice Workbook** to complete matching reviews, diagrams, calculations, and written explanations.
- In the refrigeration simulator, trace the four stages, identify condenser components, and compare hypothetical superheat and subcooling readings. Save your explanation in the workbook. The simulator's readings are examples for practice.
- Finish the online work required by your assigned plan before the supervised sessions. Record hands-on attendance through the method assigned by your instructor.

## Progress and credential evidence

The portal records completed lessons, assessments, and saved workbook responses. The instructor verifies hands-on skills and practical attendance separately. Virtual practice does not count as supervised practical hours. EPA Section 608 certification requires passing the applicable certification exam through an approved provider. Confirm exam arrangements with your instructor or learner support.
