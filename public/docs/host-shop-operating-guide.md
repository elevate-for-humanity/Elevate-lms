# Your Host Shop Operating Guide

Elevate for Humanity | Barber and Beauty Apprenticeships | November 2, 2026

## Start with the right student and program

Sign in with your own Host Shop account. Open Apprentices and verify the assigned student, location, licensed supervisor, program and authorized start date. An application alone does not authorize placement or work-hour credit. Do not create a duplicate student account.

## Keep these materials together

Use Guides & Agreements for your handbook, MOU, content-protection agreement and this guide. Open Programs and Training Schedule for your assigned syllabus, lesson order, learning objectives and apprentice progress. The signed program standard and individual training plan govern. Different occupations have different completion models; do not use one program's hour total for another.

## Every training day

- Check the assigned supervisor, safe workspace, tools and infection-control procedures.
- Review the apprentice's current theory topic. Demonstrate the related service, supervise practice and give specific feedback.
- Confirm clock-in, clock-out and unpaid-break records. Report location or clock errors for documented correction.
- Open Record Student Progress / Practical Service Log. Record the actual service, date, person or mannequin type and mentor observations. Record only work that occurred.

## Every week

- Open Work Hours, then Review Submitted Hours. Verify dates, breaks and supervised work before approving; return inaccurate records with a correction note.
- Compare actual practical work with the syllabus and the apprentice's learning needs. Agree on the next practice goals.
- Check Attendance, Training Schedule and missed lessons. Report problems through Host Shop Email.
- Keep paid workplace/OJL hours, theory/RTI and practical service counts separate. Portfolio posts do not award hours or complete formal competencies.

## Every month

Review each apprentice's progress, training coverage, supervisor assignment, wage progression and supporting payroll records. Use Reports to review source records before exporting. Elevate handles formal sponsor competency approval and training coordination; the shop provides truthful supervised-work evidence. Never restart verified student progress.

## Documents and alerts

Open Documents to see each requirement. Missing means no matching upload was located; pending means a file exists and needs review. Do not repeatedly upload a pending file unless a correction is requested. If a file was previously submitted, send its filename, approximate upload date and the screen or message showing the problem. Do not email passwords or private identity documents. Use the secure upload area.

## Invite and enroll students

Share https://www.elevateforhumanity.org/apply/student/form with prospective apprentices. They complete their own information and acknowledgments. Elevate reviews eligibility and coordinates placement, agreements and an authorized start. Ask Elevate to resolve an existing account before creating another.

## Barber & Beauty Network

Shops: open Barber & Beauty Network in your Host Shop sidebar. Students: open https://lms.elevateforhumanity.org/lms/community?network=barber-beauty. In Groups, find Barber & Beauty Network and select Join. Introduce yourself, ask for mentorship and share your own training work.

To post, write a short explanation, choose Add image, video, or PDF, add an accessibility description, review and select Post. Attachments must be 10 MB or smaller. Obtain permission before showing identifiable people. Do not post learner records, payroll, private documents or copyrighted course screenshots. Formal progress still belongs in the training logs.

## Confirm your understanding

Please reply to Host Shop Support / PARIS confirming that you can open your handbook and syllabus, identify your assigned students, approve accurate work hours, and record observed practical services. Tell us what is unclear, any dashboard error, and whether you have additional students who need enrollment help.
