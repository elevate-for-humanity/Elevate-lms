## Required rules

1. Keep the same institutional heading, section order, branding, typography, sign-off, and document delivery method across all students; vary only program-specific fields and assessments.
2. Include student name, program, provider, enrollment and report dates, concrete completed work, assessment outcomes (actual scores only when available), training hours with reporting period, current training/certification status, instructor comments, and preparer/signature.
3. Base each factual statement on documented source evidence. Preserve source/provenance and distinguish pending instructor verification **in internal records**; do not convert an unverified quiz score or study-time entry into a certified grade/approved hour. Do not invent percentages, scores, completion dates, or sign-offs.
4. Keep operational troubleshooting and learner access defects out of external academic progress reports unless specifically requested or required by a reporting obligation; track and repair those issues separately.
5. Require owner review and approval before sending. Send the approved report as an attachment or secure document link with a short email. Do not auto-send drafts.
6. Keep a copy of the approved report, evidence references, recipient, send date, and approval/audit trail in the authorized student records system; do not expose student PII in Git.
7. Apply this standard to **all future progress reports**, not only Crystal Simms.
