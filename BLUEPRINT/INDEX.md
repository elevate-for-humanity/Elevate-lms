## PLATFORM PURPOSE

Elevate for Humanity is a **workforce development platform** that guides individuals from unemployment to employment through:

1. **Career training programs** (barbering, HVAC, medical assisting, CDL, etc.)
2. **Registered apprenticeships** (earn while you learn)
3. **Government-funded pathways** (WIOA, Vocational Rehabilitation)
4. **Employer partnerships** (host shops, hiring pipelines)
5. **Testing & certification** (PSI, state boards, EPA)
6. **Career placement** (resume help, interview prep, employer connections)

---

## BUSINESS MODEL

### Revenue Streams
1. Government grants (WIOA, VR contracts)
2. Tuition payments (self-pay, payment plans)
3. Employer partnerships (host shop fees, sponsorship)
4. Testing fees
5. Store merchandise

### Target Populations
1. **Unemployed adults** seeking career transformation
2. **Underemployed workers** seeking skill upgrades
3. **Justice-involved individuals** seeking re-entry
4. **Veterans** seeking civilian careers
5. **Individuals with disabilities** (VR partnership)
6. **Single parents** seeking stable employment

### Key Differentiators
- **Apprenticeship model** (earn while you learn)
- **Government funding partnerships** (many pay $0)
- **Employer relationships** (hiring pipeline)
- **Full lifecycle support** (enrollment → graduation → placement)

Tracking: Geofence clock in/out
Approval: Host shop supervisor sign-off

- Licensed barbershop/salon
- 1:3 mentor ratio
- General liability insurance
- Workers comp
