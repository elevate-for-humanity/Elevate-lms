# Host Shop Organic Launch Pack

## Campaign rules

- Organic only: no paid Google, Bing, Meta, or display ads.
- Use public business contact information only and personalize outreach manually.
- Do not describe apprentices as free labor, automatically licensed, or guaranteed placements.
- Do not promise funding, reimbursement, revenue, rankings, calls, walk-ins, or hiring outcomes.
- Use the canonical application: `https://www.elevateforhumanity.org/partners/host-shop/apply`.
- Use city landing pages under `/partners/host-shops/indiana/[city]` and append UTM parameters to outreach links.

## Measurement without paid ads

Use unique UTM links for each channel and asset:

- `utm_source=instagram|facebook|linkedin|youtube|community|walk_in`
- `utm_medium=reel|story|post|article|dm|qr`
- `utm_campaign=indiana_host_sites`
- `utm_content=<asset-name-or-city>`

Track completed Host Site applications as the primary conversion. Secondary measures are regional-page visits, application starts, calls, map clicks, booking clicks, and qualified applications—not impressions alone.
