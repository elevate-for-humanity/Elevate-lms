My name is Elizabeth Greene. I founded Elevate for Humanity because I know what it feels like

We are not a college. We are not a staffing agency.
We are a training institute that takes you from where you are today
to a nationally recognized credential and a job offer —
with funding, hands-on instruction, and employer connections built into every program.

I am a U.S. military veteran, an IRS Enrolled Agent, and an EPA 608 Certified Proctor.
I built this platform because the people who need it most deserve the same quality
that everyone else takes for granted.

If you are ready to start — we are ready for you.
