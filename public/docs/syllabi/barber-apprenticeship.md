## Program Overview

The Barber Registered Apprenticeship combines structured Related Technical Instruction (RTI) with supervised on-the-job learning (OJL) in a participating barbershop. Apprentices build technical, sanitation, client-service, workplace, and business competencies while their verified OJL and RTI progress is documented for the registered program.

The apprenticeship is not the same as Indiana's school-based barber education pathway. School-hour requirements and registered-apprenticeship requirements must not be combined or substituted for one another.

---

## Authoritative Apprenticeship Hours

The program's centralized RAPIDS configuration is the controlling repository source for apprenticeship-hour requirements:

- **On-the-Job Learning (OJL): 2,000 hours**
- **Related Technical Instruction (RTI): 144 hours**

Individual lesson, module, competency, or practice-hour estimates are planning tools only. They do not replace the apprentice's verified OJL/RTI records or change the registered program requirements.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of the required instruction, supervised learning, and competency verification, the apprentice should be able to:

1. Apply sanitation, infection-control, and workplace-safety procedures.
2. Perform barbering services using clippers, shears, razors, and related tools safely.
3. Demonstrate haircutting, fading, tapering, shaving, beard-grooming, and client-service competencies included in the program.
4. Conduct professional consultations and maintain appropriate client records and workplace conduct.
5. Apply basic barbershop operations, scheduling, customer-service, and business principles.
6. Maintain accurate OJL and RTI records and obtain required supervisor verification.
7. Prepare for the Indiana barber licensing process applicable at the time of completion.

---

## Related Technical Instruction

RTI is delivered through the Elevate learning platform and may include:

- Indiana barber laws, rules, and professional responsibilities
- Sanitation, infection control, bloodborne-pathogen awareness, and safety
- Hair, scalp, and skin fundamentals
- Tool use, maintenance, and technical theory
- Haircutting and clipper/scissor theory
- Shaving and facial-hair services
- Client consultation and communication
- Business operations and professionalism
- Licensing-exam preparation

The official RTI completion record in the LMS controls. Course modules may be revised to maintain alignment with current program and licensing requirements.

---

## On-the-Job Learning

OJL occurs under approved supervision at a participating host shop and may include:

- Shop orientation and sanitation procedures
- Tool handling and workstation setup
- Client consultations
- Haircutting and clipper techniques
- Scissor work and blending
- Beard trimming and shaving services
- Service documentation and client care
- Shop operations, scheduling, and professional conduct
- Competency evaluations and supervisor sign-off

OJL hours count only when they meet program requirements and are recorded and verified through the approved tracking process.

## Host-Shop Responsibilities

A participating host shop is expected to:

- Maintain required business and professional licenses applicable to its operations.
- Provide a safe training environment.
- Assign qualified supervision for the apprentice.
- Provide meaningful learning opportunities within the occupation.
- Verify OJL records and competency progress accurately.
- Follow the registered program standards and applicable employment laws.
- Communicate material attendance, performance, safety, or placement issues to the program sponsor.

A shop's appearance on the website does not guarantee an individual apprentice a placement or employment offer.

---

## Apprentice Responsibilities

Apprentices are expected to:

- Attend scheduled OJL and RTI activities.
- Record hours truthfully and within the required timeframe.
- Complete assigned LMS work and assessments.
- Follow host-shop sanitation, safety, and conduct policies.
- Maintain required program documentation.
- Participate in competency evaluations.
- Communicate absences, placement concerns, or material changes promptly.
- Meet financial obligations stated in the signed enrollment agreement when the program is self-pay.

Falsifying hours, attendance, competency evidence, or other program records is prohibited.

## Licensure

Registered-apprenticeship completion and Indiana barber licensure are related but distinct processes. Apprentices must satisfy the licensing requirements in effect when they apply. Elevate does not guarantee examination passage or license issuance.

For current licensing rules, use the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency as the authoritative state source.
