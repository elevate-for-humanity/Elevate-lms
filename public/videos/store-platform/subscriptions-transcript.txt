Choose Your Plan Without Paying for Everything
PARIS AI presenter · October 25, 2026

Which subscription actually fits your business? I am PARIS, your AI presenter for Elevate for Humanity. The store currently lists Solo at twenty nine dollars per month, Business at fifty nine, and Professional at ninety nine. Enterprise is quoted separately. These are software subscriptions, separate from training tuition and separate from following this podcast. The plan page also lists optional modules. The four assistant bundle is listed at seventy nine dollars a month. Course Creation and Learning Platform is listed at seventy nine dollars a month, with twenty five hundred monthly credits. Check which base plan and add ons your selected setup requires. For example, a solo professional who needs a basic web presence has a different problem from a training provider managing learners and assessments. Start with the problem you need solved, then ask which specific capabilities are included. Check user limits, generation credits, usage charges, billing terms, and support before purchasing. A higher price does not automatically make a plan the right fit. The store advertises a fourteen day organization trial with no card required; confirm the current trial details when you sign up. Prices in this episode were checked October twenty fifth, twenty twenty six, and can change. Use the live plans link for your decision. Following Elevate on Spotify is free.
