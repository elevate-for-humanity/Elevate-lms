# Host Shop Registered Apprenticeship Operating Guide — Federal and Indiana Rules
Effective: upon sponsor approval and issuance. Drafted 2026-10-16. Sponsor: 2Exclusive LLC-S, RAPIDS 2025-IN-132301.

## Authority and order of precedence
Use each occupation's signed, registered Standards of Apprenticeship, Appendix A work process/competency schedule, Appendix B RTI outline, apprentice agreement, applicable Indiana licensing law, and federal/state wage law. This guide does not amend registered standards. Amendments require registration-agency approval (29 CFR 29.5(b)(18)).
Official sources: https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/apprenticeship/pdfs/FinalRule29CFRPart29.pdf ; https://www.in.gov/dwd/owbla/registered-apprenticeship-basics/ ; https://www.in.gov/pla/professions/cosmetology-and-barber-home/cosmetology-and-barber-licensing-information/ ; https://www.in.gov/pla/professions/cosmetology-and-barber-home/ .

## Roles
**Host shop/employer:** employ and pay apprentice; provide qualified direct supervision and safe worksite; teach supervised occupational work processes; verify actual OJL hours and demonstrated competencies; maintain payroll, work records, safety and evaluations; release apprentice for scheduled RTI; notify sponsor about absences, job changes, safety incidents, mentor changes, and separation. Never award RTI credits.
**Elevate / designated RTI provider:** provide standards-aligned technical instruction, instructors, assignments, assessments, verified attendance/RTI credit, catch-up plans and instructional support; notify host shops of upcoming assignments and progress. RTI is not the host shop's teaching responsibility unless an approved agreement explicitly delegates it.
**Registered sponsor 2Exclusive:** register agreements and changes, maintain approved standards, wage progression and apprentice records, monitor host shops, evaluate performance, verify completion and coordinate with registration agency.

## Occupational requirements — verify against signed approvals
Repository-configured RTI: Barber 260 hours; Hair Stylist/Cosmetology 154 hours; Esthetician 300 hours; Manicurist 210 hours. These are not equivalent to Indiana beauty-school education minimums. Hair Stylist is recorded as a 2,000–2,500-hour hybrid term; barber is recorded under a 2,000-hour framework; esthetics/manicuring terms require signed-standards confirmation. Indiana licensing recognizes DOL Registered Apprenticeship completion with apprentice number and certificate, with other licensing/examination requirements applicable.

## Stages (operational guidance, not a substitute for Appendix A)
0–100 verified OJL: orientation, shop rules, sanitation, safety, observation, mentor assignment, wage and timekeeping setup; deliver required prerequisite safety instruction immediately.
101–250: guided supervised foundational tasks, customer interaction, equipment handling, work-process evidence, weekly mentor checks.
251–500: structured technical skill progression, regular RTI where permitted by the registered RTI plan; first formal progress/wage review by approved milestones.
501–1000: intermediate tasks and documented competency assessments; scheduled RTI.
1001–1500: advanced occupational work, correction plans and scheduled RTI.
1501–2000 (and beyond where approved): finish remaining required OJL/competencies, RTI, sponsor evaluation, exam/license preparation.
**Critical:** Do not enforce a blanket 250-hour RTI lock until the registration agency confirms the approved RTI timing permits it. Safety and other prerequisites may be due earlier. 500-hour probation recorded for Hair Stylist is not an RTI deferral authorization.

## Pay and wage progression
Apprentices are paid employees. The entry rate must satisfy the approved occupation-specific wage schedule and all applicable minimum-wage/overtime requirements. If the approved starting rate is $10/hour, pay at least that amount (or higher applicable legal minimum) for compensable hours; commissions must still satisfy wage law. Registered standards require progressive wage increases as skills are gained; verify milestones and actual payroll before marking compliant. Never assume $10 is a universal rate for every occupation or host.

## Daily, weekly, monthly operating rhythm
Daily: apprentice records start/end times, breaks, workplace tasks and mentor; mentor supervises and flags safety issues.
Weekly: host verifies OJL entries and evidence; reviews sanitation, tool handling, technique practice, punctuality and wages; sponsor receives exceptions.
Monthly: mentor and sponsor review Appendix A work-process coverage, demonstrated competencies, RTI progress, wage progression, attendance, safety and corrective actions.
At milestone/completion: sponsor verifies signed work-process evidence, earned RTI, wages and registered requirements before recommending completion.

## Dashboard requirements
Each assigned apprentice: registered occupation, host, mentor, approved term, probation milestone, current verified OJL, RTI required/completed/remaining (read-only from Elevate), wage floor and next wage step, work-process/competency checklist, evidence, mentor signoff, last review, next action, missing documents and exceptions. Occupational competency IDs/labels must come from the signed Appendix A; examples such as clipper handling, clipper cuts, tapers, fades, shears, shaving, sanitation and consultation are not substitutes for the approved competency list. Restrict host access to their own apprentices; no host edit of RTI credits.

## Existing apprentices
Keep verified hours and valid competencies; do not restart. Audit each existing apprentice's agreement, host, mentor, payroll, OJL and RTI records. Record missed prerequisites or RTI milestones; assign a documented individual catch-up schedule and seek registration-agency guidance for deviations. Never backdate instruction or invent hours.

## New host shops
Require verified licensing, qualified supervisor, safe worksite, insurance and wage compliance, signed host agreement, orientation, work-process checklist and dashboard access before assigning apprentices. Give this guide and the specific signed Appendix A/RTI outline, require acknowledgement, then assign only eligible apprentices.
