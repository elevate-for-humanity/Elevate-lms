## Test preparation, content and assembly

### Candidate and attempt record
At registration, staff will record legal name, mailing address, contact details, requested sections and accommodation needs. Each event receives a unique session reference. The electronic answer record will include candidate identity/address, certifying-program name/address, test date and location. Course progress and practice-test scores remain separate.

### Official bank custody and import
Only questions received through EPA's authorized process will be eligible for official forms. Another provider's protected bank and Elevate practice questions will not be substituted. A named custodian records source, receipt date, bank version, question/section/topic IDs and authorized use. A second qualified reviewer reconciles choices, answer keys and topic mapping before activation. An official bank is not represented as already received.

### How forms will be generated
The proposed assembly service will use cryptographically secure random selection without replacement within each section/topic pool. A versioned blueprint distributes questions across the required topics, and each attempt retains its exact selected item versions and scoring key reference. Different candidates receive independently generated forms, with multiple forms available at each testing event. A single fixed practice form will not be reused as an official examination.

### Section blueprint
Type I: 25 Core plus 25 Type I items. Type II: 25 Core plus 25 Type II items. Type III: 25 Core plus 25 Type III items. Universal: 25 Core plus 25 from each technical type, totaling 100. Core mapping covers environmental impacts of CFCs, HCFCs and substitute refrigerants, laws/regulations and changing industry outlook. Each technical type covers leak detection, recovery techniques, safety, shipping and disposal. The mapping and form-review record will be retained, not inferred from item counts alone.

### Scope control
Universal may combine valid passing sections only after identity, proctoring and source records are checked under the approved procedure. An open-book/mail-in Core result will not qualify toward Universal. Accommodation decisions, including any oral examination, must be resolved before testing; the packet's written-request process remains in effect.

## Proctoring and examination-day procedure

### Qualification and independence
A proposed proctor supplies identity/contact information, experience, existing authorizations, confidentiality acknowledgment and conflict disclosure. Training covers identity checks, prohibited materials, continuous observation, start/stop controls, accommodations, incidents, evidence custody and candidate notices. A supervised mock session using nonofficial questions establishes actual competence; F1 records the trainer, date, findings and authorization. No proctor may know the selected questions in advance, proctor their own exam or receive a benefit based on the result.

### Before starting
The assigned proctor completes F2, confirms site/role authorization and checks formal EPA authority and bank readiness. Compare acceptable photo ID with the candidate and session; record verifier, time and outcome. Unresolved identity differences stop admission. Clear desks/whiteboards, remove or cover reference aids, secure phones and belongings outside the testing area, inspect the active computer and explain closed-book rules and emergency arrangements.

### During the examination
One qualified proctor remains physically present for the initial one-candidate event. The proctor position must preserve continuous observation. The proctor gives procedural assistance only and records start/stop times, breaks, interruptions and irregularities without circulating protected questions. No training or answer coaching occurs in the active testing space.

### Completion and interruptions
Lock the attempt after submission, count and collect all forms, answer sheets, scratch paper and notes, and seal physical materials in a session-labeled envelope. Record custodian and transfer times in F4. A network/power failure requires preservation of state and an incident record; resume only when timing and security are demonstrable, otherwise arrange a new authorized attempt. Do not reconstruct official answers from memory.

### Post-test notice
Give a paper or electronic notice naming Elevate, program contact, the actual proctor and proctor contact, plus expected results/card dates. Record notice delivery. F3 captures candidate acknowledgment and directory choice without pre-entering consent. A candidate review request goes to admissions@elevateforhumanity.org with the session reference.

## Test security and objective grading

### Access and bank protection
Official questions and keys will remain in a private server-side repository, outside course lessons, public storage, source-code fixtures and routine email. Use individual accounts and duty-based access for custodians, proctors, reviewers and records staff. A proctor receives no reusable bank/key access. The candidate receives only assigned questions, never correct-choice markers or another candidate's record. Access changes and administrative activity are logged, reviewed monthly and after staff changes, and removed promptly when duties end.

### Development evidence versus production evidence
Packet page 3 records PR 2308 and development/database checks. Pages 5-6 describe the technical-control design. Those records are not a claim of a witnessed live examination. Before activation, retain actual deployment/configuration evidence and demonstrate session ownership, denied access, private evidence access, logging, scoring, duplicate submission, locked results, review holds, issuance and paginated reporting. Verify encryption settings, authentication, secret custody and backup restoration in the deployed environment.

### Grading method
The server will grade against the attempt's frozen question/key versions and retain responses, section totals, percentages and result. The closed-book threshold is 70 percent in each required section: at least 18 correct out of 25. A high technical score cannot offset a failed Core section. Duplicate submission returns the recorded outcome. Ordinary manual-result entry cannot replace the native examination result.

### Review and incidents
Preserve original and corrected scores, the reason, authorized reviewer and time of any correction. Hold affected issuance when misconduct or a scoring defect is under review, while escalating before required result/card deadlines. Use F4 for factual observations, evidence custody, findings and EPA consultation when needed. Restrict compromised bank versions/accounts. A reviewer independent of the disputed proctoring decision handles the review. Retests are new attempts with new authorized forms; tuition or instructor preference never changes scores.

## Records, reporting and program closure

### Permanent record method
The records custodian will maintain names/addresses of all examinees, test dates/sites, requested sections, scores/results, proctor, selected item versions, responses, incidents, correction history and credential issuance. Retain required examination records indefinitely unless properly transferred. Ordinary course-data deletion rules cannot erase those records. ### Backup and recovery
The operating standard is secure daily backup, a separately protected recovery copy and quarterly restoration exercises. Retain actual configuration evidence and restoration outcomes before calling those controls implemented. Restrict access by duty, preserve access logs and protect exported candidate information. No credential, password or official answer key belongs in an ordinary emailed report.

### Semiannual report method
For each six-month period, the custodian reconciles test counts by type, passes/fails, rates and actual credentials issued with source attempts. F6 records preparer, reviewer, coverage, corrections and delivery evidence. Follow the current EPA reporting instructions and ensure EPA receipt by January 30 and July 30, starting after the first full six-month approval period. Include bank-question concerns through an appropriate secure route. Test complete pagination so export limits do not omit records.

### Closure, transfer and inspection
For voluntary closure, inventory records, find a currently EPA-approved recipient, protect the transfer and obtain receipt. Both transfer/receipt notices follow the applicable 30-day requirement and identify the receiving program. If no willing approved recipient is found, transfer to EPA; a revoked program transfers to EPA as required. F7 preserves the manifest and technician replacement contact. Make records, sites and procedures available for EPA inspection and document corrections. Display EPA's approval letter at the testing site after approval.
