SUPERSONIC FAST CASH — LEARN TO FILE VIDEO SCRIPT
"How to File Your Own Taxes — Step by Step"
Duration: 90–120 seconds
Tone: Educational, encouraging, practical

---

SCENE 1 — OPEN (0:00–0:08)
Visual: Person sitting at a laptop at home. Tax documents next to them.
Relaxed home setting. Morning light.
Voiceover (calm, encouraging):
"Filing your own taxes doesn't have to be complicated.
Here's exactly what you need to know to get it done right."

SCENE 2 — WHAT YOU NEED (0:08–0:28)
Visual: Documents laid out on a table — W-2, 1099, Social Security card,
bank statement.
Voiceover:
"Before you start, gather your W-2 from every employer, any 1099 forms
for other income, Social Security numbers for everyone on your return,
and your bank account information for direct deposit.
That's it. You're ready to begin."

SCENE 3 — FILING STATUS (0:28–0:45)
Visual: Screen recording of the DIY filing software — filing status selection.
Voiceover:
"The first thing you'll choose is your filing status — single, married
filing jointly, head of household. This affects your standard deduction
and your tax rate. If you're not sure, our software walks you through it."

SCENE 4 — INCOME (0:45–1:00)
Visual: Entering W-2 information into the software. Import feature shown.
Voiceover:
"Next, enter your income. If your employer is in our system, you can
import your W-2 directly — no typing required. For 1099 income, enter
each form separately."

SCENE 5 — CREDITS (1:00–1:18)
Visual: Software showing Earned Income Tax Credit and Child Tax Credit screens.
Voiceover:
"This is where most people leave money on the table. Our software
automatically checks whether you qualify for the Earned Income Tax Credit,
the Child Tax Credit, and other credits. These can add thousands of dollars
to your refund — and you don't have to know to ask for them."

SCENE 6 — REVIEW AND FILE (1:18–1:35)
Visual: Review screen. Person reading through their return. Clicking submit.
E-file confirmation screen.
Voiceover:
"Before you file, review your complete return. When you're ready, e-file
directly to the IRS. You'll receive a confirmation within 24 hours.
Your refund arrives in 10 to 21 days with direct deposit."

SCENE 7 — CTA (1:35–1:50)
Visual: Website on screen. DIY filing start page.
Voiceover:
"Ready to file? Start your return at supersonicfastermoney.com.
Or if you'd rather have a professional handle it, book an appointment
at any of our Indianapolis locations."

---

PRODUCTION NOTES:
- Mix of screen recordings and real footage
- Keep screen recordings clean — no test data, use realistic sample numbers
- Voiceover should feel like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporate narrator
- End card: website URL only
