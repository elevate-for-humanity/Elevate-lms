## STATE LICENSING ROADMAP

### Indiana Barber License Path
```
HEADLINE: "How to Get Your Indiana Barber License"

STEP 1: COMPLETE TRAINING
Requirements:
- 450 hours theory (Elevate provides)
- 4,000 hours apprenticeship (at host shop)
- All competencies signed off
Elevate Certification: ✓

STEP 2: APPLY FOR EXAMINATION
Application: Indiana State Board of Barber Examiners
Fee: $100
Documents: Training completion certificate, ID, photo
Processing: 2-4 weeks

STEP 3: PASS STATE EXAMINATION
Parts:
- Written exam (theory) - 70% to pass
- Practical exam (hands-on) - 70% to pass
- Location: PSI Testing Centers
- Retake: Allowed (fee applies)

STEP 4: RECEIVE LICENSE
License Number: Issued by State Board
Fee: $50 initial
Expiration: December 31 of odd years
Renewal: $50 + continuing education

STEP 5: START YOUR CAREER
You're now a LICENSED BARBER!
Eligible to work in Indiana
Option to join professional associations
```

### Continuing Education
```
REQUIREMENTS:
- 8 hours CE every 2 years
- Topics: Sanitation, law updates, safety
- Providers: State-approved courses
- Cost: ~$50-100

OPTIONS:
- Elevate CE courses (included for graduates)
- State Board approved providers
- Professional association seminars
```

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### FAQ Accordion
```
HEADLINE: "Questions? We've Got Answers."

Q: How long does the program take?
A: Most students complete in 12-18 months, working 40 hours per week. Part-time options available (18-24 months).

Q: Do I get paid during training?
A: Yes! As an apprentice, you'll earn $14-18/hour at your host shop from day one.

Q: What if I don't have money for tuition?
A: Many students qualify for WIOA or VR funding that covers tuition, tools, and fees. Check your eligibility in 60 seconds.

Q: Will I get a job after I graduate?
A: 95% of our apprentices are hired by their host shop upon completion. We also have relationships with 50+ barber shops in Indianapolis.

Q: Do I need experience to apply?
A: No experience required! We teach you everything from scratch.

Q: What if I fail the state exam?
A: We offer free retake prep courses. Most students pass on their first attempt.

Q: Can I work at a specific salon?
A: We match you based on your location preferences and our shop partnerships. You can express interest in specific shops.

Q: What if I need to take time off?
A: Life happens. We work with you on scheduling and can pause your apprenticeship if needed.

Q: What's the difference between barber and cosmetologist?
A: Barbers specialize in men's haircuts, shaving, and facial treatments. Cosmetologists cover broader services including hair coloring, nails, and skincare. Barbers typically earn more in entry-level positions.

Q: Can I do this while working another job?
A: Many apprentices work evenings and weekends. However, you need 40 hours/week at the shop minimum for timely completion.
```
