# Cosmetology Apprenticeship Theory Syllabus

## Purpose

This syllabus keeps the apprentice, Host Shop supervisor, and Elevate LMS on the same theory topic. Digital lesson completion records learning progress. Any regulated RTI credit remains subject to the applicable registered-program standard and verified engagement records.

## Weekly pacing

- Complete 3 scheduled theory hours each week.
- Do not exceed 10 theory hours in a week without a documented schedule change from the program sponsor.
- The standard schedule is Monday through Thursday, 6:00–6:45 p.m. Eastern Time.
- Complete the assigned lesson and required knowledge check during each scheduled block.
- Save work when the stop reminder arrives and continue at the next scheduled block.

## Course sequence

1. Program Orientation and Professional Standards
2. Sanitation, Infection Control, and Safety
3. Hair Care Services
4. Hair Coloring Services
5. Skin Care and Facials
6. Nail Care Services
7. Salon Business and Client Relations
8. Final Examination and State Board Preparation

## Host Shop alignment

The Host Shop dashboard displays the apprentice's current course, weekly target, maximum, and recurring theory schedule. Supervisors should reinforce the same topic during practical coaching but must not record ordinary shop work as theory time.

## Assessment and records

- Daily theory knowledge checks require the score shown in the LMS.
- Retakes are allowed when the course lesson permits them.
- Theory time, shop OJL, and competency evidence remain separate records.
- Course completion does not itself guarantee licensure; the licensing agency determines eligibility.
