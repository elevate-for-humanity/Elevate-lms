# Barber Apprenticeship Theory and Practice Syllabus

**Provider:** Elevate for Humanity  
**Occupation:** Barber — RAPIDS 0030CB  
**Sponsor registration represented in the program registry:** 2025-IN-132301  
**Registered-standard revision represented in the program registry:** July 10, 2025  
**Completion model:** Competency based — 14 verified occupational competencies  
**Required related instruction:** 260 hours  
**Apprentice-to-mentor ratio:** 1:1  
**Probationary period:** 500 hours; probation is not program completion.

The signed approved Appendix A and the apprentice's training agreement govern the registered apprenticeship. A fixed 1,500-hour school curriculum must not be substituted for the competency-based registered completion model.

## Related instruction subjects

- Barbering history and professional development
- Anatomy, physiology, and skin/nail disorders
- Hair and scalp theory, disorders, and treatments
- Infection control and bloodborne pathogens
- Haircutting theory, tool safety, and techniques
- State board laws, rules, and regulations
- Business practices
- Preparation for the state licensing examination

The complete RTI plan must account for 260 verified instructional hours. The assigned short online lessons form part of that instruction; their publication does not establish completion of the entire RTI requirement.

## Occupational competency sequence

1. Cut and trim hair using appropriate tools and the agreed design.
2. Shape and trim beards and moustaches.
3. Apply lather and perform supervised razor shaving and contour work.
4. Apply protective coverings.
5. Clean and process tools and equipment.
6. Discuss service options and needs with clients.
7. Clean facilities and work areas.
8. Maintain financial or account records.
9. Perform administrative or clerical tasks.
10. Supervise service workers within the assigned role.
11. Maintain professional knowledge.
12. Order materials, supplies, and equipment.
13. Promote products, services, and programs accurately.
14. Maintain client information and service records.

## Completion evidence

- All 14 registered occupational competencies verified by authorized personnel.
- 260 documented RTI hours satisfying the governing standard.
- Required host-shop supervision and signed training agreements.
- Employer evaluations and applicable wage-progression evidence.
- Required course assessments and practical evidence.

Wages follow the applicable registered employer wage schedule and governing requirements. Do not use generic percentage schedules as a substitute.

## Instruction, assessment, and records

Follow the assigned LMS lesson sequence and workbook. Complete each lesson's knowledge checks at the passing score shown in the LMS. Practice the corresponding skill under qualified supervision and retain the instructor's feedback and practical evidence.

Record RTI, supervised on-the-job learning, and practical competency verification separately. Video length, a workbook answer, attendance, or a passed quiz alone does not establish all required RTI hours or practical mastery. Credit requires the applicable documented instruction and verification.

Use the signed training agreement and approved individual schedule for pacing, required materials, attendance, make-up work, and payment terms. Apprenticeship tuition is self-pay unless the exact program has a separately documented authorized payment source.

Completion of Elevate instruction does not itself issue a state license. The licensing authority determines eligibility and any additional requirements. This syllabus does not establish Indiana secondary CTE course equivalency or funding approval.
